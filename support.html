家長客服 / PARENT SUPPORT

讓我們知道遇到什麼問題。
Tell us what happened.

請由家長或照顧者聯絡：miorbit.support@gmail.com

點選信箱只會開啟您的郵件 App，不會自動寄送。本網站不會自動收集或填入裝置資訊，也沒有客服表單。

您自行決定提供哪些資訊。請勿寄送兒童姓名、照片、練習者完整紀錄或眼動紀錄。若附截圖，請先遮住個人資料。

可選擇提供的排錯資訊

  • 問題發生在哪個畫面，以及您看到的情況。
  • 您自行選擇提供 App 版本、裝置型號、iOS／iPadOS 版本。
  • 不含個人資料的重現步驟，或已遮蔽個人資料的截圖。

聯絡與資料處理

來信可能包含您的 email 地址與您自願提供的內容，僅用於回覆及排錯。客服郵件由 Google 的 Gmail 服務處理；您的郵件供應者也可能處理副本。這些服務有各自的資料處理規則。請先閱讀隱私政策。

使用範圍

首版提供免費、無廣告的 Touch 練習，Touch 不使用相機，正式眼動練習及購買／恢復購買功能停用。客服不能提供醫療診斷、療效或兒童適齡已驗證的保證。若有健康或視力疑慮，請諮詢合適的醫療專業人員。

Support for parents and caregivers

Contact miorbit.support@gmail.com. The link opens your email app; it does not send automatically. This website does not automatically collect or prefill device information and has no support form.

What you may choose to share

  • The screen where the issue occurred and what you observed.
  • App version, device model and iOS/iPadOS version, only if you choose to provide them.
  • Steps to reproduce without personal information, or a screenshot with personal information covered.
Do not send children's names, photos, full practice records or gaze records. Please remove personal information from screenshots.

Your email may contain your email address and content you voluntarily provide. It is used only to reply and troubleshoot. Google Gmail handles the support mailbox, and your own email provider may handle copies under its own rules. Read our privacy policy.

The first version provides free Touch practice without advertising. Touch does not use the camera; formal gaze practice and purchase/restore-purchase features are disabled. Support cannot guarantee medical effects or verified suitability for children. Consult an appropriate health professional about health or eyesight concerns.
