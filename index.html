Information for families

This site provides FocusTrack support and explains data handling in the app, this website and support email. The first version is designed primarily around ages 6–8. This design focus is not verified age suitability, proven improvement or a medical claim.

FocusTrack does not provide diagnosis, treatment or a promise to improve eyesight. Its first-version Touch practice does not use the camera; formal gaze practice is disabled. No advertising or active in-app purchases are offered in this version.
