隱私政策
Privacy policy

更新日期 / Last updated: 2026-10-03

本政策說明 FocusTrack 首版 Touch 練習、本靜態資訊網站及客服郵件的資料處理範圍。

App 的本機資料

練習者暱稱、偏好、練習紀錄及獎勵用於裝置上的練習與回顧。請勿使用真實姓名或敏感資訊作為暱稱。歷史畫面顯示筆數上限不等於資料保存上限。

首版 Touch 不使用相機，正式眼動練習停用。舊版或開發版本留下的校正資料，不會因功能停用而自動刪除。首版無廣告，購買及恢復購買功能停用。

刪除的範圍

App 可刪除一筆有效 Touch 歷史紀錄，或經確認刪除所選練習者的 active profile 及其相關 active 資料。刪除單筆歷史紀錄會保留已取得的獎勵。若刪除失敗或結果不明，不應視為成功。

刪除不代表所有資料完全抹除。舊版或未歸屬紀錄、遷移／來源備份、隔離紀錄、已接受的校正設定、已匯出檔案、作業系統／雲端備份，以及已寄出的郵件，不在上述 active 資料刪除承諾內。

客服郵件

由家長或照顧者自行決定是否寄信。來信可能包含 email 地址及自願提供的內容，只用於回覆與排錯。裝置型號、系統及 App 版本等資訊皆由您自行選擇提供；本網站不自動收集或填入這些資訊。請勿提供兒童姓名、照片、完整練習或眼動紀錄。

客服信箱使用 Google Gmail。您使用的郵件供應者也可能保存郵件副本；各供應者依其自己的規則處理資料。本政策不承諾固定保存天數或完全清除供應者的副本。

本網站與網站代管

本網站沒有表單、分析工具、追蹤程式、廣告、外部字型或第三方 CDN；本網站程式不設定 cookies，也不使用瀏覽器本機儲存。連結僅用於頁面導覽或開啟郵件 App。

以 GitHub Pages 代管時，GitHub 作為獨立網站代管供應者可能處理網站請求資料，例如 IP 位址及技術請求紀錄，並適用其自己的隱私規則。沒有網站追蹤程式不表示所有供應者都不處理任何資料。

系統備份與平台服務

裝置或雲端系統備份、平台診斷可能受您的裝置及 Apple 設定影響。本政策不承諾資料永不離開裝置，也不承諾已從所有備份清除。

詢問隱私問題

請由家長或照顧者聯絡 miorbit.support@gmail.com。請不要在詢問中包含兒童敏感資訊。

Scope

This policy covers the first-version FocusTrack Touch practice, this static information website and support email.

Local app data

Practitioner nicknames, preferences, practice records and rewards support practice and review on the device. Avoid real names or sensitive information in nicknames. A limit on records displayed in history is not a data-retention limit.

Touch does not use the camera. Formal gaze practice is disabled. Disabling features does not automatically remove calibration data left by older or development versions. This version has no advertising; purchase and restore-purchase features are disabled.

Limited deletion

The app can delete an eligible Touch history record, or, after confirmation, the selected practitioner's active profile and related active data. Deleting one history record preserves earned rewards. An unsuccessful or unclear result is not a successful deletion.

This is not complete erasure of all data. Legacy or unassigned records, migration/source backups, quarantined records, accepted calibration settings, exported files, OS/cloud backups and already-sent emails are outside this active-data deletion commitment.

Support email

A parent or caregiver chooses whether to email us. Messages may include your email address and content you voluntarily provide, used only to reply and troubleshoot. You choose whether to provide device model, OS or app version. This website does not automatically collect or prefill that information. Do not send children's names, photos, full practice records or gaze records.

Google Gmail handles our support mailbox. Your email provider may retain copies under its own rules. We do not promise a fixed retention period or complete removal of provider copies.

This website and hosting

This website has no forms, analytics, tracking scripts, advertising, external fonts or third-party CDN. Its code does not set cookies or use browser local storage. Links navigate between pages or open your email app.

When hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub is an independent hosting provider that may process website request information, such as IP addresses and technical request logs, under its own privacy rules. No website tracking scripts does not mean no data is processed by any provider.

System backups and platform services

Device/cloud backups and platform diagnostics may depend on your device and Apple settings. We do not promise that data never leaves the device or that all backups have been erased.

Privacy questions

Parents and caregivers may contact miorbit.support@gmail.com. Do not include sensitive information about children.
